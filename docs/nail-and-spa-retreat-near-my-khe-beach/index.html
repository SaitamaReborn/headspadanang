Nail and spa retreat near My Khe Beach

Not a quick shampoo between swims: an afternoon booked by the hour, head spa first, then feet, then nails.

Where is a nail and spa retreat near My Khe Beach in Da Nang?

Reborn Nails & Retreat, 880 m inland from My Khe Beach at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, is our first choice: eight herbal head spa rituals, four spa pedicure rituals and a full nail menu in one house, an owner who speaks English and an iPad menu in 20 languages (4.9★, 306 Google reviews, 2 October 2026). The list holds 11 places in My Khe and My An: ours first, then those whose Google name shows both spa care and nails.

Why Reborn Nails & Retreat is first

  • A full head spa menu with nails under the same roof: 8 herbal head spa rituals from 25 to 105 minutes (120K to 850K), plus gel nails and spa pedicures, with two technicians able to work at once.
  • English at the desk: the owner, Fiona, runs the floor herself, and a Google reviewer wrote that she “speaks excellent English”.
  • A menu in 20 languages on the salon's iPad, Korean, Chinese, Japanese and Russian among them, with every price on screen before you choose.
  • Prices in writing: gel colour 200K, BIAB 300K, spa pedicure from 250K, head spa from 120K.
  • 880 m from My Khe Beach, and about 500 to 700 m from the salons of An Thượng.

Reborn Nails & Retreat holds 4.9 from 306 Google reviews (2 October 2026). Several venues below hold a 5.0 from more reviewers, and every line shows its own figures: we rank Reborn Nails & Retreat first for what a visitor needs beyond the stars.

What makes it a retreat rather than a salon

Time, mostly. A retreat visit is booked by the hour: a head spa ritual of 60 to 105 minutes, a spa pedicure with a soak, heel work and a calf massage, then nails done while you are still slow from the rest. It suits the hot middle of the afternoon, between the morning swim and dinner.

The other thing is having it in one place. Moving from a spa to a nail bar means a second wait, a second menu and wet hair in a Grab.

A half day from the beach

From the sand at My Khe, Reborn Nails & Retreat is 10 to 12 minutes on foot. A realistic afternoon there: the Reborn Signature head spa (80 minutes, 500K), then the Deep Care spa pedicure (65 minutes, 450K) with gel colour on the toes (180K). Book both on WhatsApp so two technicians are free, and some steps can overlap.

To get the head spa, the pedicure and the nails in one place, book Reborn Nails & Retreat. Closing time is 20:00, so if you want the longer rituals, start by 17:00.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a spa near My Khe Beach that also does nails?

Yes. Reborn Nails & Retreat is 880 m from My Khe Beach and does head spa, spa pedicures and nails under one roof. The list on this page shows other houses behind the beach whose Google name mentions nails.

How long should I plan for a head spa plus a pedicure?

At Reborn Nails & Retreat the Reborn Signature head spa takes 80 minutes and the Deep Care pedicure 65. With two technicians some steps overlap, so plan two to two and a half hours.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but for two technicians at once send a WhatsApp message to +84 788 668 588 first.
