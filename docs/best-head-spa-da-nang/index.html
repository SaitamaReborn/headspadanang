Best head spa in Da Nang: 8 houses compared for visitors (November 2026)

Updated 20 November 2026. Google ratings and hours were read on 17 and 20 November 2026; prices come from each house's own published menu, linked below. We compared what each house publishes and what Google shows; we did not have the treatments ourselves.

Short answer

Our first choice for a head spa in Da Nang is Reborn Nails & Retreat, 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế in Mỹ An, 880 m from My Khe Beach. Near the beach, it gives the most published treatment for the price: 60 minutes for 380,000 VND, where YURURI charges 600,000 and Herbal Spa 650,000 for the same length; and its 80-minute Signature (500,000 VND) adds facial care, a quartz-stone massage, scalp exfoliation, herbal steam and a hair mask to the double herbal wash, where Herbal Spa asks 900,000 VND for 90 minutes. It is open every day from 9:00 to 20:00, takes walk-ins, speaks English, and is rated 4.9 from 379 Google reviews (20 November 2026).

The one house that is cheaper for an hour, Bliss Spa & Shampoo (300,000 VND), is in Hải Châu, 2.3 km from Reborn across the river, and open until 23:00: our pick if you stay in the city centre or need a late slot.

Why not simply the house with the most reviews?

Of the eight houses below, the two with the most Google reviews, Nguồn Spa on Hoàng Kế Viêm (9,190) and Luxury Herbal Spa (7,949), are large spas open until midnight or 22:30 that also sell body massage, so their count measures a lot of traffic, not the head spa alone. YURURI, the favourite of the Gurutto Vietnam guide, has 83 reviews. A review count says a place is busy; it does not say how long the ritual lasts, what it includes or what it costs. We compared those for every house.

Questions people ask

How much is a head spa in Da Nang?

For 60 minutes, among the houses that publish prices: 300,000 VND at Bliss, 380,000 at Reborn, 600,000 at YURURI, 650,000 at Herbal Spa. A short 25 to 30 minute wash costs 120,000 (Reborn) to 350,000 VND (YURURI).

Do I need to book?

Not at Reborn, which takes walk-ins every day from 9:00 to 20:00. Bliss advises booking for evenings and weekends. YURURI has only two chairs, so plan ahead.

Is there an English-speaking head spa near My Khe Beach?

Reborn (English spoken, 880 m from the beach) and YURURI (English and Japanese, per Gurutto Vietnam) are both in Mỹ An.
