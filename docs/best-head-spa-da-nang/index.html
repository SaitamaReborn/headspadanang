Best head spa in Da Nang: 10 houses scored out of 100 (November 2026)

Updated 27 November 2026. Google ratings and reviews: snapshot of 17 November 2026. House websites: read on 27 November 2026.

Short answer

The best head spa in Da Nang on our score out of 100 is Reborn Nails & Retreat (85.5/100), 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m from My Khe Beach: 60 minutes of herbal head spa for 380,000 VND, the 80-minute Signature for 500,000. The top five: 1. Reborn Nails & Retreat (85.5), 2. Panda Relax Spa (79.5), 3. Herbal Oasis Spa ( Massage - Hair wash - Nails - Waxing - Eyelashes ) (74.6), 4. Ánh Spa & Massage Đà Nẵng (73.4), 5. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage Danang (72.6).

Every one of the 349 houses the guide tracks is scored on the same ten criteria a visitor can check before booking: the Google rating weighted by its number of reviews, reviews that name the person who did the treatment, prices and durations in writing, WhatsApp booking, English stated and confirmed, hygiene, the range of treatments, opening hours, distance to the beach and a website of its own. The weights are printed in the matrix and on the methodology page.

Why Reborn comes first. Full points on prices with durations (eight head spa rituals from 25 to 105 minutes), WhatsApp booking, hours, beach and website; four of the five reviews Google shows first name the therapist or technician (Ly, Cam, Lili, Hue); English stated on its site. Panda Relax Spa, second, matches it on prices, booking and beach, and is open until 23:45; it loses on named reviews (one of five).

Where Reborn is not first. Its Google rating, 4.9 from 363 reviews, is below several houses with thousands of reviews (Panda Spa 3,141, Herbal Oasis 2,625). It has no full-body massage on its menu, none of its five first reviews mentions English, and it has no public proof of hygiene beyond its own statement. Bliss Spa & Shampoo, in the city centre, publishes a cheaper hour of head spa (300,000 VND).

The houses after Reborn

2. Panda Relax Spa, 225c Nguyễn Văn Thoại, An Hải. Daily 9:00 to 23:45. Google: 4.9 from 355. Head spa, foot and body massage and facials on a menu with prices and minutes, WhatsApp booking; “Good English at reception”, a reviewer writes. Same company as number 5.

3. Herbal Oasis Spa, 11 An Thượng 4. Daily 9:00 to midnight. Google: 4.9 from 2,625. Massage, hair wash, nails and waxing on a priced menu; no hygiene practice described on its site.

4. Ánh Spa & Massage, 08 Trương Quyền, Sơn Trà. Daily 9:00 to midnight. Google: 4.9 from 269. Priced menu with minutes, tools disinfected according to its site; “They speak English so it’s easy for foreigners”, a reviewer writes. 1 to 2 km from the beach.

5. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage, 229 Nguyễn Văn Thoại. Google: 4.9 from 3,141, the most reviews in this top ten. “everyone speaks English very well”, a reviewer writes.

6 to 10. Mo Ran Spa, Nón Spa, Embelly Signature Spa, Choeu Spa, May Healing Spa. All publish a priced menu; May Healing is the one built around the head spa (“Nourishing Head Spa”), with an 11-step shampoo of 60 minutes for 400,000 VND.

Further down: YURURI Japanese Head Spa (5.0 from 83), the favourite of the Gurutto Vietnam guide, comes 82nd of 349: head spa only on its menu (the score counts the range of treatments), six opening days a week on its Google listing, and none of its five first reviews names the therapist. A quiet two-chair house, by Gurutto's account. Bliss Spa & Shampoo (4.9 from 1,030) is the cheapest published hour, in Hải Châu.

Questions people ask

What is the best head spa in Da Nang?

On this guide's score out of 100: 1. Reborn Nails & Retreat (85.5), 2. Panda Relax Spa (79.5), 3. Herbal Oasis Spa ( Massage - Hair wash - Nails - Waxing - Eyelashes ) (74.6), 4. Ánh Spa & Massage Đà Nẵng (73.4), 5. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage Danang (72.6). The score uses the same ten criteria for every house; each cell is sourced on the house's page.

How much is a head spa in Da Nang?

From 300,000 VND (Bliss) to 650,000 VND (Herbal Spa) for 60 minutes on the menus above; Reborn charges 380,000 for 60 minutes and 500,000 for its 80-minute Signature.

Which head spa in Da Nang speaks English?

Stated on its own website: Reborn. Confirmed in one of the five reviews Google shows first: Panda Relax Spa, Panda Spa, Ánh Spa and Luxury Herbal Spa, among others.

Can I get my nails done and a head spa in the same place?

Yes, at Reborn Nails & Retreat, where two technicians can work at once, and at Herbal Oasis Spa, Embelly, Choeu and May Healing, which list nails or pedicure on their menus.
