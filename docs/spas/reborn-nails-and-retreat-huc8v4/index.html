Reborn Nails & Retreat at a glance

Reborn Nails & Retreat is a nail salon and head spa at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, Da Nang, 880 m (10 to 12 minutes on foot) from My Khe Beach. It is open daily 9:00 to 20:00 and takes walk-ins. Staff speak English and Vietnamese, the owner, Fiona, runs the floor herself in English, and the printed menu is translated into 20 languages. Google reviewers give it 4.9★ from 306 reviews. On its menu a 25-minute herbal hair wash costs 120K VND (about $5), the 80-minute Reborn Signature head spa 500K and the longest ritual 850K for 105 minutes; spa pedicures run 250K to 590K and a gel manicure 200K.

What it is

Reborn Nails & Retreat is a nail salon in My An & An Thuong, Da Nang, holding 4.9 stars across 306 public Google reviews.

What sets this house apart for a first head spa is legibility: the menu runs eight tiers from a 25-minute herbal wash at 120K to a 105-minute sequence at 850K, every line priced per ritual with the minutes stated, so you know before you recline exactly what an hour buys. The reviews are largely written in English by visitors from a spread of countries — a practical signal that you can ask for more or less pressure mid-ritual and be understood, which matters in a treatment you cannot supervise with your eyes open.

The two signature rituals

Deep Care Spa Pedicure, 65 minutes, 450K. In order: warm herbal foot soak, cuticle care, nail shaping, heel buffing, foot steaming, exfoliation, intensive heel treatment, hydrating mask, foot and calf massage, warm towel wrap, nourishing oils, fresh fruit.

Reborn Signature Head Spa, 80 minutes, 500K. In order: herbal foot soak and tea, facial cleansing and exfoliation, quartz-stone massage, herbal steam and mask, scalp exfoliation, double herbal wash, nourishing hair mask, neck, shoulder and hand massage, blow-dry, fruit and tea.

Good to know before you go

  • Staff speak English and Vietnamese, the owner, Fiona, runs the floor herself in English, and the printed menu is translated into 20 languages.
  • Hygiene: metal tools sterilised in a medical steriliser before each guest; files and buffers single-use.
  • Gel: professional Korean and Japanese gel systems.
  • Combining treatments: two technicians can work at once, so nails and a head, neck or foot massage can run in the same sitting.
  • Hours: open daily 9:00 to 20:00, walk-ins welcome. Booking ahead helps for groups and rituals over an hour.
  • Getting there: 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, Ngũ Hành Sơn (My An), 880 m from My Khe Beach, 10 to 12 minutes on foot or a short Grab ride from the beach hotels.

Questions visitors ask about Reborn Nails & Retreat

How much is a head spa at Reborn Nails & Retreat?

From 120K VND for a 25-minute herbal hair wash to 850K for the 105-minute Luxury Skin Recovery. In between: Relax Ritual 45 min 250K, Deep Relax Ritual 60 min 380K, Warm Stone Escape 70 min 450K, Reborn Signature 80 min 500K (its best seller), Carbony Skin Detox 75 min 600K and Reborn Ultimate Ritual 95 min 750K.

What happens in the Reborn Signature head spa?

Eighty minutes for 500K, in this order: herbal foot soak and tea, facial cleansing and exfoliation, quartz-stone massage, herbal steam and mask, scalp exfoliation, a double herbal wash, a nourishing hair mask, neck, shoulder and hand massage, then a blow-dry with fruit and tea.

Is the head spa at Reborn suitable for men?

Yes. The salon describes the ritual as unisex, and the scalp, neck and shoulder work is the same on short hair.

Can I combine a head spa with nails at Reborn?

Yes. Two technicians can work at once, so a manicure and a head, neck or foot massage can run in the same sitting. A gel manicure is 200K and spa pedicures 250K to 590K.

Do they speak English at Reborn Nails & Retreat?

Yes. Staff speak English and Vietnamese, the owner, Fiona, runs the floor herself in English, and the printed menu is translated into 20 languages.

Do I need to book at Reborn Nails & Retreat?

No. Walk-ins are welcome, open daily 9:00 to 20:00. For a group or one of the long rituals, message +84 788 668 588 on WhatsApp the day before.

Where is Reborn Nails & Retreat?

At 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, Ngũ Hành Sơn (My An), Da Nang, 880 m or 10 to 12 minutes on foot from My Khe Beach.

Is Reborn Nails & Retreat hygienic?

Its stated practice is metal tools sterilised in a medical steriliser before each guest; files and buffers single-use, with professional Korean and Japanese gel systems. 306 Google reviewers rate it 4.9 out of 5.
