Wet season · 7 min · 18 August 2026

The wet season is the head spa's season: Da Nang, September to December

Da Nang's rainy season by the numbers, what 85 percent humidity does to a scalp, why the blow-dry outranks the herbal steam in November, and how to book around an afternoon downpour.

In short
  • The rain is scheduled, not constant. October carries the most wet days of any month in Da Nang's climate record · around 24 · and it falls in bursts weighted to the late afternoon and evening.
  • Humidity is the scalp problem, not the rain. Heat plus sebum plus trapped sweat feeds Malassezia, and the flakes it produces are greasy rather than dry. The response is washing more, not less.
  • In November the blow-dry stops being a courtesy and becomes the thing you paid for. The herbal steam, meanwhile, is the worst-value upgrade of the year.
  • Book mornings and book close. The rain arrives with the afternoon and takes the motorbike taxis with it.

The rain arrives on a schedule

Da Nang's wet season runs roughly September to December, and its shape is more specific than the phrase suggests. September is the hinge: long-run averages put it near 309 mm, third wettest of the year, which sounds worse than it behaves. October and November are the months that earned the reputation. Climate archives disagree on the totals · normal for a coastal city with a mountain range at its back · with one widely used monthly series putting November near 596 mm across about 23 wet days and other summaries pushing both October and November past 600. They agree on the ranking, and they agree on the more useful number: October carries the highest count of rainy days in the calendar, somewhere around 24.

What a monthly total hides is the timing. Wet-season rain here mostly does not fall as a grey all-day drizzle. It arrives in bursts, heavily weighted toward the late afternoon and evening, and then it stops and the street steams. Layered on top of that, from October into December the city sits in the path of tropical depressions and typhoons coming off the East Sea · a different category of weather entirely. Those days flood the low ground, close roads and are not days for planning anything.

Take the storm days out and what is left is a season where the sky is reliably against you for two or three hours somewhere between 15:00 and 20:00, and largely indifferent the rest of the time. Mỹ Khê spends stretches of both months under red flags with the sea too rough to swim, which removes the one activity most visitors built the trip around. That is a scheduling problem rather than a ruined holiday, and it happens to align with an hour you were going to spend indoors anyway.

What eighty-five percent humidity does to a scalp

The monthly humidity series for Da Nang sits between roughly 75 and 85 percent across the year and peaks in the wet months; one climate summary puts November near 90. Numbers in that range are why the second week of a rainy-season trip often feels different on the head than the first · heavier at the roots by mid-afternoon, itchier at the crown, and flaking in a way that was not happening at home.

The mechanism has nothing to do with rain landing on you. Malassezia is a lipophilic yeast living on essentially every adult scalp; it feeds on the lipids in sebum, and the dermatology literature ties its overgrowth to the flaking, itch and redness of seborrhoeic dermatitis. Heat, sweat and the occlusion of hair against a damp scalp raise oil output and tilt surface conditions in the yeast's favour. That is the standard explanation for why dandruff flares when people move to the tropics, and why the flakes here tend to be greasy and yellowish rather than dry and white.

Two honest sentences before anyone reads a cure into that. A head spa is not a treatment for seborrhoeic dermatitis · an antifungal shampoo with ketoconazole or zinc pyrithione, sold over the counter at any Long Châu or Pharmacity in the city for less than a mid-tier ritual, is · and no herbal decoction has been tested head-to-head against one. What the ritual does have going for it is the least glamorous variable in the whole field: wash frequency. Guidance for humid climates runs toward washing more often, not less, and the wet season is precisely when visitors wash less, because hair that will not dry is hair you stop touching. Trà xanh, green tea, is the note Da Nang blends lean on for oily scalps and the one worth asking for in October · the herb breakdown covers what else is in the pot. Da đầu mình bị dầu · my scalp is oily · is enough to get the blend switched at the counter.

The blow-dry stops being a courtesy

In March you can leave a spa with damp hair, walk fifteen minutes along the beach road and arrive dry. In November you cannot, and the reason is physics rather than inconvenience: evaporation needs a humidity gradient, and at ninety percent there is barely one. Hair that leaves the building damp stays damp for hours, sits warm against the scalp under a hotel air-conditioner, and undoes a good part of what you just paid for.

So treat the finish as a specification, not a flourish. A proper one is ten to fifteen minutes of hot air and a round brush, and it is the difference between the ritual ending well and ending with a towel in your room. Check it is in the package before you recline, because the 25-minute entry wash at 120K sometimes stops at a towel dry. Sấy thật khô giúp mình nhé · please dry it really thoroughly · is the whole request, and asking for it at the counter rather than at the end is what makes it land.

Which brings the tier argument, and I will make it bluntly: the herbal steam is the worst value on the menu in November. The 70-minute warm-stone ritual around 450K and the 80-minute signature at 500K both layer in a lemongrass steam, and in February it is one of the finest things in the city. In a month where you have been walking through ambient steam since breakfast, you are paying 70K to 120K extra for more of the exact sensation you came indoors to escape. Warm stones survive the argument · that is dry heat on a cold, wet-shouldered afternoon, and it holds up. But the 60-minute tier at 380K, with the double shampoo, the neck and shoulder work and a full dry, is the wet-season sweet spot, and the money saved buys a second visit rather than a wetter first one. The full ladder is on the tier table.

Rain shrinks the map

A 700-metre walk to An Thượng costs nothing in March. In an October burst it is the entire plan, and the burst does not give notice. The practical consequence is one every wet-season visitor meets sooner or later: motorbike-taxi supply collapses at exactly the moment the rain starts, because the drivers are getting wet too, and Grab cars go to surge pricing with a queue behind them. A ten-minute ride across the Dragon Bridge becomes forty.

So book the house you can reach on foot in bad weather rather than the one you liked the look of on the map. If you are on the river side, that means Hải Châu and the streets around Hàn Market; if you are near the beach, Mỹ An and An Thượng. What you should not do in October is book a 19:00 slot on the opposite bank. The neighbourhood guide lays out which cluster runs which clientele, and in the wet season proximity should outrank almost everything else on that page.

Two counter-side details make the rest easier. Ask them to call you a car before the blow-dry, not after · Gọi giúp mình xe Grab nhé · so the driver arrives as you finish rather than ten minutes into you standing near an open door in a draught. And in a storm week, message the day before rather than assuming: houses do close when the street outside floods, and the 80-minute signature that ends at 21:30 is the booking most likely to strand you.

The best month of the year to book

The wet season is low season, and low season is where the arithmetic turns. The Friday and Saturday evening pressure that makes a walk-in fail in July eases considerably once the beach stops working; the rooms in Mỹ An that turn couples away in high summer take them at 19:30 in late October. Nothing about the ritual is worse. The decoction is the same decoction and the hands are the same hands, and you are simply not competing for them.

The morning window doubles down too. Our standing position is that 09:00 to 11:00 is the best-value hour in Da Nang · fresh decoction, quiet room, a technician who has not yet worked ten hours. In the wet season it is also the most reliably dry part of the day. Book 09:30 and you have spent the one dependable clear window on something indoors, which sounds like a waste until the 16:00 downpour arrives and you are already dry, styled and drinking artichoke tea instead of standing under an awning on Nguyễn Văn Thoại.

So, plainly: October is the best month of the year to have a head spa in Da Nang. It is the only recommendation in this guide that improves as the weather gets worse · cheaper flights, quieter rooms, a scalp that genuinely needs the attention, and an afternoon with nothing competing for it. If you have not been through the sequence before, the walkthrough covers what arrives when, and the honest floor for a wet-season visit is 45 minutes at 250K. Below that you are buying a wash you could have had at your hotel sink, and the point of the season is that you have the time.

Frequently asked

Is Da Nang worth visiting in the rainy season?

For anything indoors, yes, and it is materially cheaper. The rain concentrates in bursts from mid-afternoon into the evening rather than falling all day, so mornings usually hold. What you lose is the beach · Mỹ Khê spends stretches of October and November too rough to swim · and the risk you accept is the typhoon and flood window running October into December.

What is there to do in Da Nang when it rains?

Museums, the covered markets, coffee houses and the city's spa hour. A head spa is the strongest fit because it is 45 to 80 minutes fully reclined and indoors, priced at 250K to 500K, and the wet months are low season, so rooms that turn walk-ins away in July have space on a Friday evening in October.

Why is my scalp oily and flaky in Vietnam?

Heat and humidity raise sebum output, and sweat trapped against the scalp favours Malassezia, the lipophilic yeast behind seborrhoeic dermatitis. The result is greasy yellowish flakes rather than dry white ones. Wash more often rather than less, and if it persists, an antifungal shampoo from a Vietnamese pharmacy is the actual treatment · a head spa is not.

When is the rainy season in Da Nang?

Roughly September through December. September is transitional at around 309 mm. October has the most rainy days of any month, near 24, and October and November carry the heaviest totals · one commonly cited series puts November close to 596 mm. Tropical depressions and typhoons are possible from October into December.
