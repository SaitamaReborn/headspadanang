Where to go

The tradition is everywhere in Da Nang. The standard is not.

Which area of Da Nang is best for a head spa?

Hai Chau (103 houses), My Khe beachfront (56 houses), My An & An Thuong (45 houses). My An and An Thượng hold the densest cluster with English menus; Hải Châu serves a mostly local clientele at gentler prices with some of the most practised hands in the city; the beach road charges for its postcode. Across the city 247 houses carry a public Google rating with at least twenty reviews.

Read the menu first

Houses worth your hour price per ritual, by length, with the minutes stated — "Deep Relax · 60 min · 380K". Menus that itemise the wash, the massage and the dry separately produce bigger bills and choppier experiences. Cross-check against our fair-rate table.

Then the room, then the hands

Towels folded fresh, loungers wiped between guests, combs and razors from sealed packs, and air that smells of herbs rather than chemicals. And pace: the shampoo takes as long as the shampoo takes. If the first five minutes feel rushed, the next fifty-five will too. The full list is on how to choose.
