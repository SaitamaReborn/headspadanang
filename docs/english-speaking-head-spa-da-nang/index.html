Why language matters more for a head spa

Pressure, water temperature, whether you want the facial steps, which oils go on a sensitive scalp: all of it is decided before you recline. Ten seconds of English at the desk saves an hour of guessing, and it is the part of the visit you cannot fix halfway through.

What to say before you lie down

Mention a sensitive or sunburnt scalp, coloured hair, or a stiff neck from the flight. Ask to skip the face mask if you want to keep your make-up, and say if you would rather not have your hair blow-dried.

How we built the list

For each of the 294 houses in this guide, we read the Google reviews returned (up to five per house, snapshot of 2 October 2026) and kept those where a reviewer says the staff speak or understand English. A house absent from the list may well speak English too. That only means no reviewer in that sample said so. The editors place our pick first.
